# September 18th: The PCB

Hello guys! Welcome to my BLARE project, my first thing I did was create my pcb.
My PCB design knowledge is very limited, with a random youtube tutorial being my only pretext, so I did what any reasonable person would do and follow the guide!
I decided that 4 buttons was good enough to do what I wanted. How did I reach this conclusion? My casio watch!
My watch has 4 buttons that control mode, light, set, and add time. I realized that I could port these over.
I finished up my PCB, and then added footprints!
